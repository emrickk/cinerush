CineRush Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026 · Portal Interactive, Inc.

This policy describes how the CineRush app for iPhone and iPad ("the app") handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information. It has no user accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking, and it does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts.

Information kept on your device

The app saves your settings and game choices (for example control options, music volume and selected car or track) only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app.

Apple services

If you install the app from the App Store or TestFlight, Apple may provide us with aggregated crash and usage reports according to the sharing choices in your device settings. Apple's handling of that data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
